Background
03
Background
Cancer cells rewire metabolism under PI3K, MEK and TAK1 signaling
PI3Ki+MEKi and PI3Ki+TAKi inhibit AGS growth synergistically
Gap: expression analysis gives broad labels, not metabolic detail
Question: which metabolic tasks change, and which only in the combination?
The system
AGS gastric adenocarcinoma cells
PI3K inhibitor PI-103 (PI3Ki)
MEK inhibitor PD0325901 (MEKi)
TAK1 inhibitor (5Z)-7-oxozeaenol (TAKi)
Two combinations: PI3Ki+MEKi, PI3Ki+TAKi
Control: DMSO; 4 replicates per condition

Term
Meaning
GPR rule
Complex = AND (take min); isozymes = OR (take largest |log2FC|)
pFBA
Flux balance analysis picking the smallest-total-flux pathway per task
FVA
Flux range per reaction; 149 of 187 tasks have alternative pathways
Permutation null
Shuffle gene log2FC 10,000 times; significant if tail fraction &lt; 0.025
GI50
Dose that halves growth; singles at GI50, combinations at half of each
Jaccard index
Set overlap: intersection divided by union (0 to 1)
